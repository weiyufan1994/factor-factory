# Factor Forge Fundamental Domain

Give Step2 a focused specialist proposal, not another copy of the research loop.
Activate from the hypothesis/estimand, not the presence of a familiar data column.
State an out-of-domain or underspecified question honestly.

## Model the question

Read the actual source, current hypothesis, mathematical object, information
set, horizon and specific question. Preserve the frozen estimand; a proposed
change is a separate revision for the research owner to consider.

Specify the economic driver, accounting/value relation and market-pricing bridge.
DCF, residual income, accounting identities, structural/causal models and other
tools are choices justified by this hypothesis, not a universal valuation recipe.

Compare the preferred model with a real null/alias and material rivals. Publish
definitions and decisive derivation steps, then the observation equation,
retained/lost information, predicted signature and a distinguishing test.
Use the study's [content rules](../factor-forge-ultimate/references/compatibility-constraints.md);
one suitable method can be enough.

Bind observations to publication dates, revisions/restatements, units, reporting
periods and legal availability. Explain proxy/identification error rather than
silently substituting a convenient field for the selected estimand.

Choose operator, direct-code or hybrid implementation after modeling.
Send unresolved availability to Data Liaison and measurement changes back to Step2.
Similar operator names or good historical returns do not establish equivalence.

## Return usable advice

Provide the proposed object/equations, assumptions, data/proxy needs, applicability,
falsifier and specific unresolved issues. Cite prior cases as analogies or
counterexamples with provenance, not proof for this factor. An ordinary advisory
memo belongs at the assigned workspace path; it does not alter canonical Step
artifacts, execute code or approve a branch.

For an explicitly assigned signed organization task, read
[hosted proposal](references/hosted-proposal.md) and follow that task's exact
envelope/admission rules. Independence is an actual separate context, not a role
label. The host-mode format is not a prerequisite for ordinary domain advice.
