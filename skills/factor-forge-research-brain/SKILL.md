# Factor Forge Research Brain

## What This Skill Does

This skill adds the **investment logic layer** on top of the Factor Forge pipeline.
It is not a raw execution step. It is the thinking framework that should guide:
- Step5 case closing,
- Step6 reflection,
- Step6 revision proposals,
- and promote / iterate / reject decisions.

Use this skill when a factor already has Step4 evidence and we need to answer:
1. how the factor is supposed to make money,
2. whether the edge is risk premium, information advantage, or constraint-driven arbitrage,
3. what objective constraints force the other side to behave predictably,
4. and what kind of revision would strengthen the real return source rather than cosmetically improving metrics.

## Judgment Responsibilities

Exploration and explanation may follow the question; this is not a fixed thinking
order. At review, verify and preserve the hypothesis, estimand, information set,
model/measurement program and implementation identity frozen before evaluation.
Do not reselect them after seeing results or reconstruct missing preregistration.

- Check evidence quality and authority before relying on a conclusion. Interpret
  metrics against frozen predictions, separating information, tradability,
  diagnostics and promotion evidence.
- Investigate failures before proposing a bounded revision. Distinguish a defect,
  a contradicted claim and an unresolved explanation; do not manufacture a payer,
  new mechanism or branch. New model selection belongs to an explicit revision.
- Record supported lessons, conditions and uncertainty. No new idea is a valid
  research outcome; current nonempty learning/idea-seed field checks may still
  block formal landing, as documented in the current research contract.
- Recommend only the decision and authority supported by the active Step6,
  Council, OOS, human-approval and proof contracts. Free exploration does not
  change evaluation-before-conclusion or preregistration-before-results rules.
