# Optional source-reading questions

Use the questions relevant to the source; record accepted fields using
[schema](schema.md) and [local intake](local-intake.md), not a second template.

## Primary and challenger

Read the whole source and recover its final construction and components.
Where are sign, windows, aggregation order, normalization and weighting defined?
Which economic, behavioral and causal claims are explicit, and which are your
reconstruction? Which missing convention could materially change the estimator?

Give the challenger the original source in a distinct context, not the primary's
answer. Ask it to find omissions, alternative readings and decisive source
evidence. Independence does not require a different provider.

## Chief adjudication

Compare both original readings against the source. Resolve material conflicts
with cited evidence and preserve unresolved ones, rather than blending them
into apparent agreement. Record accepted/rejected components and final assembly.
Confidence describes the evidence; it does not establish alpha strength.

## From an economic question to a mathematical object

What economic state, constraint, relation or parameter is the formula measuring?
Under which assumptions does it imply a value/payoff/price/return response?
What does the observation or transformation retain, lose or alias? Which actual
null or rival mechanism could produce the same observation, and what test would
separate it? Keep unexplained payer/payoff links explicit.

An equation may be an identity, institutional constraint, behavioral model,
empirical invariance or conjecture; label it honestly. Use tools that fit this
question, not a fixed list. A mathematical analysis of the source is not authority
to substitute a new detector for the source baseline.

When separately asked to discover a new factor, explain its equation, assumptions,
observable estimator, prediction, falsifier and complexity/cost limits. Retain
the candidate as a proposal until the existing research loop selects that branch.
