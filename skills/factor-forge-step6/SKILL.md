# Factor Forge Step6

Step6 owns the research judgment and next question, not just a status summary.
Consume the frozen source/spec/measurement program, actual Step4/5 results,
journal, code-review evidence and applicable prior knowledge. Execute through
Ultimate using the study's current workspace and pin.

## Interpret, reflect, decide

- Start with what was actually tested. For replication distinguish the source
  algorithm, reconstruction assumptions and independent extensions.
- Compare predicted and observed signatures. Separate a contradicted prediction
  from a demonstrated cause, an implementation/data defect and an unidentified
  explanation. Retain plausible competitors and surprises.
- Distinguish signal information, gross long-side return and after-cost account
  results. A useful feature can survive an unprofitable implementation; a good IC
  does not prove a payer or tradable alpha.
- Choose the next action for research value and cost. Revising the mechanism,
  estimand or response structure requires a new Step2 induction. An implementation
  repair returns to Step2/3 review. Stopping or preserving an unknown is legitimate.
- Extract supported lessons, failed ideas, reuse conditions and counterexamples.
  New ideas and retrieval hits are useful only when they change a concrete
  question, test or interpretation. Empty results carry reasons.

The reasoning order is flexible. Preserve the pre-result hypothesis, information
set and evaluation design when judging evidence. Required tests should distinguish
the claim, not satisfy a prose-length or branch-count target.

## Deliver and continue

Write a public research memo: assumptions, derivation summary, evidence,
counterarguments, limitations and decision rationale. Keep the original metrics
and identity visible. The memo is not hidden chain-of-thought and cannot replace
missing execution or independent review.

A revision identifies the failed layer, changed mathematical term, preserved
invariants, distinguishing prediction/test, budget and stop rule. Keep parent
results and use the existing child-approval path. Council is for substantive
disagreement or a protocol requirement, not every routine edit; independent
routes need actual separate contexts, not author role-play.

Use the existing decision values `reject|iterate|needs_human_review|promote_official`
within their supported scope. Ordinary IS can close a supported local rejection;
it cannot grant official promotion. Report the economic conclusion, native
execution/validation status and independent evidence separately. Preserve
useful partial evidence without certifying an incomplete account.

Ultimate handles routine continuation and the journal handoff. A missing
reviewer, invalid evidence or exhausted budget remains explicit. Local lessons
are knowledge-maintenance candidates, not self-published canonical conclusions.
