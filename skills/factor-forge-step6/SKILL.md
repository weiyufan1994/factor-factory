# Factor Forge Step6

Step6 judges research and controls continuation. It consumes the frozen subject,
spec/measurement program, Step4/5 artifacts, journal or independent researcher
memo, knowledge provenance and protocol records. It does not manufacture missing
metrics or turn a completed wrapper into formal proof. Execute through Ultimate.

## Research judgment

Start with the question the study actually tested. For source replication keep
source construction, reconstruction assumptions and extensions separate. Compare
expected and observed evidence with the economic hypothesis, mathematical object,
market-outcome map, observation/estimator, implementation and trading economics.
The order of explanation is flexible; the authority of frozen pre-result choices
is not. Do not reverse-engineer a mechanism from attractive returns.

Reflect openly: retain plausible competitors, rejected analogies, surprises and
unknowns. No fixed mathematical family, search method or novel branch is required
by the research question. A valid outcome is rejection, useful but limited
information, or “available evidence cannot identify the explanation.” Do not
invent a payer, cause, new law or idea seed to make the case look complete.
Current artifact field/count restrictions remain explicit compatibility limits,
not a reason to pad the research; see the reference below before formal landing.

Separate signal ordering, information content, gross long return, after-cost
long/relative performance, volatility, drawdown/recovery, capacity and evidence
quality. A metric may challenge or support a prediction without identifying its
cause. No long-short/short-leg adoption, decile trading, portfolio-policy rescue
or outcome-driven parameter changes. Only `risk_premium` makes broad bucket
monotonicity and Fama–MacBeth formal obligations.

For anomalies, distinguish an implementation/spec/data defect from an empirical
failure and an unresolved observation. Return suspected defects through Ultimate
to Step2/3; withhold affected conclusions until re-review and new tests. Weak
returns alone are not a bug. Revising the estimand creates a new hypothesis,
not a silent repair. Keep observed results and failed branches visible.

## Decision and continuation

Write a substantive memo explaining what survived, what failed, evidence limits,
reusable conditions/counterexamples and the next discriminating question if any.
Preserve the external researcher context, loop brief and provenance. A local
candidate lesson is not canonical knowledge, and an empty retrieval is not a ban
on new mathematics.

Use `reject`, `iterate`, `needs_human_review` or `promote_official` only within the
active contract. Local IS can reject under its existing terminal rule without
manufacturing signed OOS proof; it cannot promote officially. A justified revision
names the failed layer, changed mathematical term, invariants, prediction,
falsifier, trial boundary and stop rule. Preserve the parent and use the existing
approval/child path before execution; an advisory proposal is not authority.

Council is appropriate for substantive unresolved revision questions or a frozen
protocol requirement. It is not automatically a multi-person ceremony for every
routine edit. Use actual independent agents where independence is required;
sequential self-analysis and mocks cannot meet that requirement. Internal memo/
Council checkpoints are continued by Ultimate within scope; unavailable channels,
missing evidence or budget exhaustion remain explicit blockers, not fake results.

Report economic judgment, local execution, native protocol/validator status,
Council evidence and formal authority separately. Step6 cannot self-certify
independent acceptance or promote its own memory candidate.
