# Step6 current operating contract

Read for ordinary judgment and field landing. For Council, EVO/children or formal
proof use the conditional references in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md); their authority
is preserved but is not a local prerequisite.

## Inputs and evidence

Consume exact manifest-selected run/case/spec masters, Step5 handoff, Step4/5
metrics/charts/backend evidence, researcher journal or memo, knowledge provenance,
state/conjecture/approaches. Revision also needs obligation and counterexample
ledgers; formal promotion/final approval needs the formal certificate. Missing
required evidence is BLOCK, never a fixture/narrative fallback.

Preserve existing researcher memo under `research_memo.researcher_agent_memo`
and journal under `research_memo.researcher_journal`. Normal research requires
one of these real external research contexts; script-only analysis is insufficient.
Identity mismatch, unusable backend evidence, illegal information or unresolved
implementation risk must not be disguised as economic rejection or promotion.

## Judgment record

`research_memo` records formula understanding, return-source hypothesis, evidence
quality, metric interpretation, failure/risk analysis, decision rationale and
bounded next tests. Preserve the frozen measurement program in mechanism analysis,
revision hypotheses when present and the loop brief. Required record surfaces:

- `math_discipline_review`: selected object/estimand, information legality, spec
  stability, signal/portfolio gap, revision implication, generalization/overfit
  assessment and kill criteria;
- `learning_and_innovation`: supported patterns, anti-patterns, actual prior
  lessons, idea seeds if warranted and reuse boundaries/instructions;
- `experience_chain`, `revision_taxonomy`, `program_search_policy`,
  `diversity_position`, plus evidence audit, mechanism analysis, case comparison,
  revision/search decisions;
- `loop_research_brief__<report_id>__iter<n>.md/json`, linked from
  `research_iteration_master.loop_research_brief`: economics, metrics/charts,
  knowledge comparison, next direction and conclusion.

Unknown or not-applicable research content must be explicit and evidence-bound;
do not fabricate learning or run search to fill a field. Some consumers still
require fixed nonempty collections/method keys: [compatibility constraints](../../factor-forge-ultimate/references/compatibility-constraints.md)
records the exact limitations. Formal validation may BLOCK when a reasonable
unknown cannot be represented; do not silently weaken or bypass it.

`validate_step6.py` returns `PASS|WARN|BLOCK`; a BLOCK precludes claimed native
closure/promotion. `information_set_legality=illegal*`, missing kill/reuse criteria,
and unknown overfit risk for promotion are substantive gates. Preserve upstream
legacy math only when already present; never synthesize it. Do not emit
`dd_view_edge_trade`, which belongs to company diligence.

## Economics and claim scope

Interpret metrics against preregistered model predictions. A positive IC does
not prove a payer or profitable high-score long leg; gross positive return does
not prove after-cost edge. Long-short/decile charts are diagnostic-only and must
be labeled accordingly. Non-risk-premium bucket shape is diagnostic, not a
universal adoption rule. Preserve valid ties rather than manufacturing buckets.

Existing working defaults are candidate long-side Sharpe >= 0.50, official >=
0.80, drawdown soft limit -35%, recovery soft limit 252 trading days, and trading
cost `turnover * 0.3%` absent a better frozen cost model. These are recorded
policies, not retrospective knobs or self-sufficient admission proof. Volatility
is risk-capital pressure; `-0.5 * sigma^2` is a log-growth diagnostic, not direct
trading cost or a required stochastic core model.

Preserve the exact study window and evaluation design. Current minute IS defaults
to ending `2025-07-11`; later held-out data cannot become a revision reward.
Missing/invalid derived state is a data/production-path BLOCK, not a mechanism
rejection. No portfolio/rebalance/short-leg repair of factor adoption.

Keep `narrative_only`, `math_framed`, `metric_candidate`, `metric_consistent`,
`component_validated`, and optional `stochastic_validated|payer_validated`
distinct. `metric_consistent` needs accepted replayable factor proof;
`component_validated` also needs trusted measurement/ablation evidence. Missing
trusted payer/stochastic verifiers means retain falsifiable research, not a
passed obligation. Stochastic qualification is not a universal ladder stage.

## Local terminal path

For ordinary `--local-is-only`, existing code supports a bounded rejection only
after the actual researcher and independent reviewer agree to reject the same
identity, with substantive review and no requested modification. The record has
`local_is_authority_boundary`, `decision=reject`, no approved revision/handoff,
no branches and no official write. This is economic/local closure, not signed
factor-proof REJECT. The local exception does not skip state/conjecture/approach
`pre_council` checks or permit reconstructing them after the outcome. Let the
actual wrapper/Step6 validators decide native status and report any remaining
BLOCK separately from the economic conclusion.

Do not force a Council/child proposal solely to reject a completed local attempt.
If revision is warranted or Council is active, read
[Council and revision](council-and-revision.md). That formal terminal-rejection
route has different certificate and dispatch requirements. Local IS cannot
issue OOS release, verified Host authority or `promote_official`.

## Validation, revision and writeback

The existing protocol gates are `pre_council` before Council (and local Step3–5),
`pre_revision` before revision, `pre_promotion` before any official write, and
`final` for final synthesis. Use the wrapper for formal execution. For bounded local repair/debug at `pre_council` or `pre_revision`, the validator
CLI is:

```sh
python3 scripts/validate_factorforge_research_protocol.py --local-is-only \
  --workspace-root <factor_workspace> --report-id <report_id> \
  --stage <pre_council|pre_revision>
```

Use the active local scope when applicable; never invoke a hosted gate to invent
local authority. A revision changes the declared mathematical layer, preserves
unaffected invariants and parent evidence, and states distinguishing tests/kill
criteria. Changed estimand = new hypothesis. Execution uses the existing human
approval and child lineage contract; Council prose cannot approve itself.

Every real attempt belongs in the full experiment record, including blocked or
rejected work with evidence boundaries and reopen conditions. Only verified
promotion enters the official library. Local lessons/candidate exports are
advisory; repository-root writeback follows the explicit existing export path.
See [knowledge maintenance](../../factor-forge-ultimate/references/knowledge-maintenance.md)
at completion/interruption. Hosted role-memory review remains a distinct signed
independent-review/promotion process in [hosted organization](../../factor-forge-ultimate/references/hosted-organization.md).

Keep research-protocol PASS, Council validity, factor certificate verdict,
EVO terminal state, independent memory review, external human approval, child
execution and canonical promotion separate. `NO_DERIVED_LAW` may be a valid stop
with factor verdict `NOT_ISSUED`; it is not an invented ACCEPT/REJECT certificate.
