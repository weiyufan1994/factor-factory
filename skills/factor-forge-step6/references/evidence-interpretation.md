# Interpreting factor evidence

Read for Step5/6 economic judgment. Start from the frozen hypothesis and
mathematical predictions, then inspect the actual observations and their limits.
This is research guidance, not a mandatory menu of models.

## What could produce the return?

| Hypothesis | Useful distinguishing evidence |
|---|---|
| Risk premium | Compensation for bearing a named risk; exposure and cross-sectional pricing. Broad monotonicity and Fama–MacBeth are formal obligations only for this claim. |
| Information advantage | Earlier/cleaner information relative to consensus; the specified long-end or conditional response, timing and decay. Smooth ordering in every bucket is not universal. |
| Constraint-driven arbitrage | A named actor/action/constraint, payoff transfer and repeatability; concentration when the constraint binds and disappearance when it does not. It need not be risk-free. |
| Mixed or unidentified | Which signatures each rival explains, what is not identified and the next observation that could separate them. |

A plausible payer is a hypothesis until tested. IC, a weak short leg or an
attractive story cannot identify it. Use legal proxies and negative controls
appropriate to the claim. Missing evidence may leave the mechanism unresolved.

## Information and investability are different

Inspect the fixed-direction IC/rank IC, conditional/group gross evidence and
long-end outcome before interpreting turnover and costs. Then examine the
actual account path, benchmark, fees, drawdown/recovery, liquidity and capacity.
High trading cost can reject the current implementation without erasing
information in the signal; useful outcomes include a feature or state descriptor.

Retain the current long-only mandate. Long-short/decile charts are diagnostics,
not adoption evidence. Keep valid ties and actual sample sizes. Overlapping
forward-return labels are not independent daily account returns.

Use the study's frozen costs, windows, universe and evaluation policy. Existing
working thresholds belong to the [operating reference](current-operating-contract.md),
not post-result tuning. Volatility is risk-capital pressure; `-0.5*sigma^2` is
a log-growth approximation, not an extra trading fee or an obligation to use an SDE.

## Why did the prediction fail?

First distinguish code, data, time alignment, measurement/specification and
empirical prediction failure. Return a reproducible implementation suspicion
through Ultimate to Step2/3; preserve the original evidence and retest the repair.
When the calculation is sound, compare material rival mechanisms rather than
calling the most fluent explanation identified.

Audit the mathematical object, assumptions, observation equation and information
lost by transformations. Use component ablation, negative controls, limiting
cases or regime comparisons where they distinguish the question. A regime label
chosen after seeing returns is a new hypothesis, not confirmation.

A new response structure needs forward mathematical induction: assumptions →
implications → observable estimator → discriminating prediction. It may yield an
identity, non-identification or no return direction. SDEs, spectral methods,
projection and other tools are choices, not required ornaments. Use the
[self-improving reference](../../factor-forge-ultimate/references/self-improving-research.md)
for candidate classification and the implemented search limits.

## Decide what survives

State whether evidence supports framing, a metric association, a tested component
or a causal/payer claim. Formal certificate labels retain their existing
requirements; ordinary diagnostics do not become certified through eloquent prose.
A stochastic or Dirac-style claim needs its actual assumptions, derivation,
limiting cases, information-loss audit and falsifiers, not just the label.

Each extra state, parameter, interaction or data dependency needs a mathematical
role and marginal evidence. Complexity is a cost, not an arbitrary primitive
count. A bounded search can implement or challenge the derivation; its best score
does not retrospectively supply that derivation.

Preserve supported lessons, counterexamples and transfer boundaries. A similar
historical case is a prior, not evidence for this identity. Explicitly record
which question or test changed after retrieval. No derived law or reusable
lesson can be the honest conclusion. Record it and stop when the budget or
evidence no longer justifies another branch.

For frozen EVO or formal promotion, use the separate mode references in Step6;
their qualification, OOS and canonical-memory rules are not ordinary IS prerequisites.
