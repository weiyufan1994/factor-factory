# Factor Forge Ultimate

## Role

Ultimate is the formal deep-research orchestrator. It combines the Step skills,
the current main agent's research judgment, dynamic Council routes, executable
evidence, and durable knowledge writeback.

Miner is a separate candidate factory. A report-led or named factor idea may
enter Ultimate directly; do not force it through Miner.

## Entry Routing

1. Identify whether the request is intake/formalization, formal execution,
   Step6/Council review, EVO V2 control, or historical compatibility work.
2. Read the required references and paired skills before mutating anything.
3. Use the exact current CLI and authority boundaries from the operating
   contract. Do not reconstruct a command from memory.
4. Preserve one workspace, manifest identity, legal-time/data provenance,
   evidence boundaries, and disjoint assurance meanings through completion.
5. Stop only at a terminal outcome, an explicit user-requested pause, or a true
   BLOCK that cannot be resolved within current authority.
