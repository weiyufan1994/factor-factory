# Authoring research records

Read when creating/revising the formal state, conjecture or approach registry,
not on every code/debug step. The current agent owns economics and mathematics;
scripts validate/materialize, never invent hypotheses, actors, falsifiers or
public derivation. For exact fields read
[the protocol](../../../docs/contracts/factorforge-research-conjecture-protocol-v1.zh-CN.md)
and [compatibility constraints](compatibility-constraints.md). The protocol's
OOS/Miner sections apply only to those modes; local scope retains its IS policy.

Freeze the research question, claim class, estimand, legal information, source
versus reconstruction assumptions, null/rivals, and terminal criteria. Preserve
open mathematical tool choice; valuation, stochastic, causal, spectral and new
objects are possibilities, not requirements. An observation map may be identity;
do not manufacture latent states. Public records contain definitions, decisive
derivations and uncertainty, not private chain-of-thought.

Before implementation, bind the selected mathematical object, mechanism,
market-outcome projection, observation equation, measurement components,
information retained/lost, applicable audits and discriminating tests through
[the measurement program](../../../docs/contracts/mechanism_conditioned_measurement_program_v1.zh-CN.md).
Freeze trial/multiplicity budget, IS boundary, applicable purge/embargo,
evaluation design, costs/capacity and stop conditions before viewing results.
Do not select `risk_premium` after attractive buckets; only that claim class
adds Fama–MacBeth and broad bucket-monotonicity proof obligations.

For ordinary local IS, materialize only real agent-authored inputs:

```sh
python3 scripts/write_factorforge_research_protocol.py --local-is-only \
  --workspace-root <factor_workspace> --report-id <report_id> \
  --state <state.json> --conjecture <conjecture.json> --approaches <approaches.json>
```

The wrapper applies `pre_council` before local Step3/3B/4/5 and Council. Revision,
promotion and final synthesis use their respective protocol gates. Missing or
stale pre-result records are not retrospectively reconstructed as blind research.
The local writer/validator flag is `--local-is-only`. Hosted scope uses its
own frozen authority contract; do not infer hosted approval from this example.

Use the existing journal/state machine to identify current owner, question,
blocked/reopen condition and evidence. Routes are generated by the problem,
not fixed personnel. Current minimum counts and field roles are real consumer
constraints listed in the compatibility reference; if a defensible study cannot
satisfy them, report that conflict instead of inventing distinct hypotheses.
