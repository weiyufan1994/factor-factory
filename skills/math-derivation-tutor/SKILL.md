# Math Derivation Tutor

Use this repository candidate after the responsible researcher has written a
concrete derivation or a focused mathematical question. It is not a mandatory
call for every factor, a classroom workflow, a formal proof system, or an
independent research-review identity.

Audit the smallest material issue: definitions and domains, information timing,
assumptions behind each key step, transformation/limit behavior, correspondence
between equation, estimator and code, or what a failed prediction can and cannot
identify. State the conclusion, unresolved condition and the smallest
discriminating check. Preserve Unknown rather than filling a missing bridge.

For a new response structure, the research owner must still complete the
mechanism-first induction in
[the Ultimate reference](../factor-forge-ultimate/references/self-improving-research.md).
For Step3 code, audit actual reviewed code and its invoked helpers; do not infer
implementation behavior from a formula alone. A Tutor audit may find an issue,
but it does not approve Step3 or replace the distinct Step2 review.
